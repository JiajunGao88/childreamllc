About Childream LLC

We believe that sports are not just about strengthening the body, but about shaping character. At Childream LLC, we are committed to creating a training environment that balances challenge with care, guiding children to grow through experience. We provide structured, professional basketball programs for children of all abilities, welcoming both typically developing children and those with developmental needs such as Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), helping each child find their own rhythm and path forward.

Every training session is more than just the accumulation of skills; it is a journey where children build resilience through persistence, learn communication through teamwork, and develop confidence through overcoming challenges. Our programs are carefully designed by experienced coaches, combining technical skill development with motor coordination, social engagement, and emotional growth to support well-rounded development.

On the court, under the rhythm of the game and the warmth of the sun, we witness growth, celebrate progress, and walk alongside each child as they become their best selves.

Our Programs

Comprehensive training programs designed for children of all abilities, including specialized support for those with developmental needs.

Basketball Training Program

Develop fundamental basketball skills while enhancing social interaction and confidence

+

Program Overview

Our Basketball Training Program develops fundamental basketball skills while enhancing social interaction, physical coordination, and confidence. Sessions are tailored to each participant's ability level, ensuring progress in dribbling, shooting, passing, defensive skills, and game strategies. The program also emphasizes teamwork, sportsmanship, and discipline, making it suitable for both typically developing children and those with developmental needs.

Private 1-on-1

Highly personalized training focused on individual skill development and goals.

Small Group

Collaborative training in a small group setting, promoting teamwork and peer motivation.

Adaptive Physical & Fitness Training Program

Designed for children of all abilities with diverse physical, developmental, or behavioral needs

+

Program Overview

Adaptive Physical & Fitness Training is designed for children of all abilities, including those with diverse physical, developmental, or behavioral needs. Led by highly trained instructors, sessions focus on improving gross motor coordination, balance, core strength, endurance, and body awareness. Activities may include functional strength exercises, agility drills, stretching, and sensory-motor games, all aimed at promoting attention, self-regulation, and overall confidence in a safe, supportive environment.

Private 1-on-1

Individualized training plan tailored to specific physical and developmental goals.

Small Group

Group setting (2–4 participants) that encourages peer interaction while maintaining targeted instruction.

Social Games & Cognitive Skills Program

Build expressive language, attention, memory, problem-solving, and turn-taking skills

+

Program Overview

The Social Games & Cognitive Skills Program uses structured, engaging activities to build expressive language, attention, memory, problem-solving, and turn-taking skills. Led by experienced instructors, sessions incorporate board games, role-play, and visual prompts to support both social communication and cognitive development. This program is ideal for both typically developing children and those with diverse learning needs.

Small Group

Small group format (2–4 participants) encouraging interaction and cooperation while targeting cognitive skill growth.
